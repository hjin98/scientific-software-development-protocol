## Measurement contract

This is deterministic static source measurement, not live model/harness telemetry. Each trace fixes its task predicate before measuring exact UTF-8 Git-blob bytes in the accepted Protocol 6.2 recovery and the Protocol 6.3 candidate. The active sets are semantic routing hypotheses derived from the role/router predicates and are independently challengeable; package membership and ordinary Markdown links are not treated as activation.

## Interpretation

- The first-clean/local and other non-memory-triggering traces keep PEM cold; raw project history is not eagerly replayed.
- Historical recovery where prior choices matter and mature replacement with demonstrated project history add the PEM owner as a bounded conditional read.
- The larger bytes of a memory-triggered trace are not a regression by themselves: the trigger deliberately buys access to project learning when it can change the decision.
- Static active-set/byte evidence is structural only. No live-context or performance claim is made.
- These measurements do not establish semantic correctness of HAS, causality, maturity, authority binding, or evidence admissibility; those remain separate Stage-F qualification obligations.
