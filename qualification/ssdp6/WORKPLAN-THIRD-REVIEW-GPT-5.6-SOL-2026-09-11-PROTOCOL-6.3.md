## Remaining blocking findings found and closed

### R63-WP-26 — Positive-family admission could cherry-pick favorable history

**Defect:** the prior workplan required counterevidence preservation once a `SUCCESS_PATTERN` existed, but initial promotion could still select only favorable examples and ignore materially applicable older failures/neutral results.

**Repair:** admission/material strengthening now requires a bounded evidence-directed counterevidence search over the declared coverage/aggregation scope before `SUPPORTED`/`PROVEN` positive guidance. Search basis and material blind spots remain explicit; incomplete search keeps the candidate provisional/review-required.

**Disposition:** CLOSED.

### R63-WP-27 — Hot/active-summary emphasis could become the applicability boundary

**Defect:** the prior HAS wording explicitly dispositioned intersecting Hot families, risking omission of a Warm/Cold lesson that is more relevant to the current change.

**Repair:** after PEM activation, bounded applicability matching uses owner, surface, mechanism, capability, regime, and task predicates across current PEM metadata. HAS includes every materially relevant entry surfaced or independently known regardless of temperature. Temperature affects attention order only.

**Disposition:** CLOSED.

### R63-WP-28 — Statistics could pool materially incompatible regimes

**Defect:** project-wide occurrence/application counts could combine different languages, backends, scales, lifecycle contexts, owners, or implementation classes and produce a misleading generalized lesson.

**Repair:** each statistical family now declares an aggregation scope. Materially different regimes are stratified or split unless the generalized claim explicitly spans them and evidence supports pooling. Aggregate history cannot erase subgroup interpretation.

**Disposition:** CLOSED.

### R63-WP-29 — Evidence/reference rot could leave stale active knowledge

**Defect:** durable evidence routes were required, but the prior plan did not define what happens when a material external/protected/authority binding later becomes unavailable or uninterpretable.

**Repair:** material routes now have binding-health semantics. Event/activation-driven checks mark affected current use review-required/unavailable and remove unqualified positive guidance when its warrant is no longer sufficient. Historical claims are retained; unaffected evidence is preserved. No background full-memory scan is required.

**Disposition:** CLOSED.

### R63-WP-30 — Authority-bound memory lacked reverse impact closure

**Defect:** an `AUTHORITY_BOUND` capability could become stale after its cited D1-D4/project owner changed, even if the PEM file itself was untouched.

**Repair:** accepted owner changes must perform bounded reverse impact closure over materially affected PEM authority bindings/current notices, confirming, remapping, downgrading, retiring, or challenging each affected relation. Existing search/dependency mechanisms are reused; no universal reverse-index database is introduced.

**Disposition:** CLOSED.

### R63-WP-31 — Failure families could suppress safe/disconfirming evidence

**Defect:** negative families counted confirmed failures but did not require material safe counterexamples that falsify an overbroad cause or applicability claim to remain visible.

**Repair:** material disconfirming/safe-regime evidence now constrains family cause, scope, aggregation, and applicability without subtracting real occurrences. Overbroad families must narrow/split/reclassify.

**Disposition:** CLOSED.

### R63-WP-32 — Current notices had expiry text but no fail-closed state transition

**Defect:** a notice could retain active guidance after its review/expiry condition became true.

**Repair:** expiry/review triggers now force the notice out of unqualified active guidance into reconciliation, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, or retirement. Enforcement is event/activation-driven rather than a new daemon.

**Disposition:** CLOSED.

### R63-WP-33 — Quantitative optimization evidence could be materially confounded

**Defect:** effect size and experimental envelope were preserved, but the prior plan did not explicitly require comparator equivalence/normalization or adequate treatment of measurement noise. A hardware/cache/concurrency change could be misattributed to the optimization under study.

**Repair:** quantitative claims now preserve baseline/candidate identity, workload, hardware/backend/precision/toolchain, concurrency/resources, cache/warm-up/checkpoint state, measurement definition, normalization, and uncertainty/replicates where material. **Disposition:** CLOSED.

**Defect:** temperature, maturity, and counterevidence were separate, but a high-temperature success family with unresolved material contradiction or broken warrant could still be rendered inside positive guidance.

**Repair:** positive-guidance eligibility is now explicit. Only current, sufficiently supported, healthy, non-materially-contradicted bounded claims may be phrased as recommended project instincts. Contested history may stay salient, but as uncertainty/caution.
