# Protocol 6.5 Cross-Model Adjudication

## 1. Disposition

The frozen Opus 5.5 Phase I-III diagnostic is accepted as a valid defect-discovery result, but its thirteen findings are not adopted one-for-one as successor requirements.

The cross-check against the accepted Protocol 6.4 repository state and the historical GPT-5.6 Sol Review sequence supports a Protocol 6.5 successor. The successor is justified by four coupled defect families in SSDP self-governance, not by a defect in the D1-D4 scientific/software authority model.

No Serious Challenge is raised against the accepted D1-D4 abstraction/concretization model, evidence model, or PEM non-authority boundary.

The second independent frontier diagnostic is intentionally skipped for this cycle because of frontier-model resource limits. This does not turn repeated same-model reasoning into cross-model independence. The reserved clean branch remains available for later replication.

## 2. Cross-model evidence result

The strongest cross-check is the relationship between the GPT-5.6 Sol 6.4 Reviews and the Opus mutation probe.

GPT-5.6 Sol repeatedly found local lifecycle/qualification-oracle defects (B64-R4..R9), strengthened the QF64 counterfactual matrix, and finally concluded that the repaired matrix had adequate discriminating polarity. Opus then mutated the real canonical protocol source rather than the synthetic oracle inputs. The complete documented mechanical acceptance workflow detected only 3/15 semantic inversions while rejecting 3/3 meaning-preserving paraphrases.

This establishes a higher-order defect: the old qualification machinery was effective at checking its own represented fixture vocabulary but weakly bound to the semantic subject whose correctness it purported to qualify.

The result does not imply that arbitrary prose semantics should be mechanically theorem-proved. It implies that mechanical evidence must make narrower claims, while semantic Review must attack the assembled protocol itself rather than treating a synthetic obligation matrix as a proxy for semantic adequacy.

## 4. Admitted defect families

### DF-1 — Self-hosted release-state and version-lifecycle architecture

Accepted-current/recovery/candidate/public-fallback facts are copied by value into versioned semantic source, generated prompts, role metadata, documentation and workplans. Immutable fallback source therefore contains lifecycle statements that become stale by construction.

Required successor invariant:

**Version-intrinsic protocol semantics and mutable release-state facts are distinct semantic objects with distinct owners. Immutable version-bound source must not claim mutable current lifecycle truth except as explicitly time-scoped historical snapshot information.**

One current release-state owner must be sufficient to determine accepted-current version, exact fallback mapping, recovery mapping and ratification state. Secondary current surfaces are references or generated projections, not independent value copies.

The public-source fallback remains distinct from recovery, but final fallback identity is published only after its source semantics have passed independent assembled-candidate Review and required stakeholder ratification.

### DF-2 — Qualification and Review epistemology

Protocol 6.4 blurs three different evidence claims:

1. structural/executable consistency;
2. semantic adequacy of the protocol text;
3. improved engineering outcomes.

Required successor invariant:

**Every qualification result states the exact property and subject it discriminates. Structural tests cannot be presented as semantic proof; semantic Review cannot substitute an author-supplied obligation matrix for inspection of the assembled protocol; performance claims require outcome evidence.**

QF-style synthetic predicates may remain only for properties they actually implement. Exact prose wording is tested only when the wording/token itself is a machine/public contract.

Independent Review must contain a bounded out-of-matrix pass asking what locally compliant failure remains possible outside the author's declared decomposition.

### DF-3 — Meta-control semantics and governance

High-leverage protocol predicates and gates are partly implicit.

6.5 must define, at one canonical owner:

- **material/materially** — decision-local significance tied to a plausible change in governed interpretation, admissibility, evidence applicability, acceptance/reopen state, risk or protected outcome; speculative remote possibility alone is insufficient;
- **independent Review/falsification** — a reviewer/context that did not author the candidate and does not inherit author conclusions; model-family diversity is stronger corroboration but not mandatory independence;
- **Serious Challenge threshold** — concrete contradiction, counterexample, materially consequential ambiguity, incompatibility or evidence-backed reason the accepted owner may be inadequate; mere possibility is insufficient;
- **Challenge resolution** — owning authority resolves it; a clarification that narrows materially admissible behavior is a semantic mutation for dependency/evidence purposes unless proven representation-only;
- **protocol-version acceptance** — technical Review PASS makes a candidate eligible; explicit human/stakeholder ratification establishes the protocol-version acceptance decision; automation may publish/represent that decision but cannot manufacture it;
- **accepted PEM publication/basis** — selected by explicit project integration policy, not inferred from file presence, branch tip or stale self-description.

### DF-4 — Representation, schema and convergence self-application

SSDP already requires one owner, progressive disclosure, schema/source authority, simplification after recurrence and lossless representation, but its own release evolution violates these rules.

Required successor invariant:

**SSDP development is itself governed by the current accepted SSDP ownership/evidence/representation/convergence rules, except for explicit bounded bootstrap exceptions required by version transition.**

6.5 must integrate version-labelled 6.4 amendment sections into current canonical owners, remove stale predecessor labels from current templates/entrypoints, eliminate duplicated generic activation predicates where a route suffices, and reconcile PEM doctrine/template/validator semantics.

Compression must preserve capability; line count alone is not an oracle.

## 5. Benchmark-design correction discovered during adjudication

The preregistered benchmark contains one material methodological defect: requiring the ordinary mechanical acceptance workflow to detect a strictly larger fraction of arbitrary semantic prose mutations incentivizes exactly the phrase-pinning or semantic-parser machinery that A-02 shows is misaligned.

The corrected evaluation separates:

- **mechanical mutation tests** for machine-readable/current-state/schema/generated/parity invariants;
- **semantic mutation/counterexample review** against the assembled protocol by an independent reviewer/context;
- **outcome comparison** only for the claim that 6.5 improves engineering behavior.

A second correction is proportional replication. Fixed three-fold replication for every task/model is not a meaningful universal statistical rule and violates evidence-value discipline. Paired trials are required; replicate stochastic or ambiguous cases until the decision is stable enough for the bounded claim rather than mechanically repeating every case three times.

These corrections are made before P1 design/performance is observed and therefore do not rescue a failing candidate.

## 6. Version classification

The admitted work is a backward-compatible strengthening of protocol self-governance, qualification semantics, meta-control definitions and representation discipline. It preserves accepted D1-D4 domain semantics and therefore fits **Protocol 6.5 minor** rather than a major redesign.

A separate 6.4.x patch is not required if 6.5 proceeds immediately. If 6.5 is materially delayed, A-01's current-state contradictions should be repaired independently as a 6.4.x correctness patch without waiting for the successor.

## 7. Cross-check conclusion

Protocol 6.5 is warranted.

The successor should not be thirteen patches. It should repair four owner-level causes:

1. release-state/version lifecycle;
2. qualification/Review epistemology;
3. meta-control semantics/governance;
4. representation/schema/convergence self-application.

A-13 remains deferred. Protocol 7 D3 architecture remains outside scope.
