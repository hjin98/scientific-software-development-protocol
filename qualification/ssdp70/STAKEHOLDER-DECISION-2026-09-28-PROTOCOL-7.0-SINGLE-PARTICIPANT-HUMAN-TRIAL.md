# Protocol 7.0 human legibility trial — stakeholder as single participant

Stage F stopped at entry (`STAGE-F-ENTRY-STOP-2026-09-28.md`). The frozen contract §7 required at least four independent human participants, and the implementing context could not supply them. The stakeholder responded: "Let me perform the minimal required human trial that is necessary."

Asked which design to use, the stakeholder selected **"You alone"**:

- the stakeholder is the only participant;
- the stakeholder reviews one fixture per arm, never the same fixture twice;
- each arm keeps at least 20 routine and 4 critical questions;
- every other floor is unchanged;
- the stated limitation is that a pass establishes legibility for the stakeholder-reader only.

**Scope.** This is a governed change to the Stage A qualification contract. It is not a workplan change: workplan §11.6 already names "the stakeholder, or a designated scientist representing the declared reader", requires at least one fixture from each arm, and counterbalances only "where more than one participant exists".

The change replaces only the contract's participant-count and counterbalancing clauses. Per-arm answer exposure, the critical and routine floors, the time bound, the custody rules and the release-blocking rule are unchanged. The earlier recheck PASS binds only the prior contract bytes, so the revised contract needs a fresh independent check before any candidate run.
