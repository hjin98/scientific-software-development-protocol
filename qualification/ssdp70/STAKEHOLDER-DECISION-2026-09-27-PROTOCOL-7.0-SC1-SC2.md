# Stakeholder Decision — Protocol 7.0 SC1 and SC2

The stakeholder, as owner of the consolidated workplan's §4 obligation-binding rule, dispositioned the two Serious Challenges raised by the `c50f267` workplan-level Review.

## SC1 — Channel B claim integrity: fix ACCEPTED

§4 permits an always-binding **claim-integrity floor** for agent-authored assertions in any authorized deliverable, including product reports: no claim stronger than its evidence, and no exploratory or selected result presented as confirmatory or pre-specified. The floor is satisfiable by qualifying, narrowing or withholding the claim and never adds a product capability. Everything else in Channel B remains O3-only. The floor replaces the inconsistent special cases in §4.3 (materiality disclosure inside product reports) and §6.2.2 ("is a defect").

## SC2 — O1 to O3 conversion: Option B SELECTED

O1 splits into:

- **(i)** material RSR, intended reader and the routine questions the reader needs answered — always required when the predicate fires;
- **(ii)** the **product inspectability surface**: which of those questions the product itself must retain, project or expose, marked as such in the authority. "Answered through agent reports only" and "None" are valid.

A marked (ii) item beyond the requested deliverable binds as O3(a) only after acceptance by the owner of product scope (stakeholder or task authority); technical D3 Review alone does not suffice. An applicable D1/D2 human gate satisfies this when it considers the marked surface. Until accepted, the item stays proposed while the rest of the authority may proceed.

The stakeholder's rationale: this adds architecture-level transparency — the authority shows which product capabilities exist for inspectability and who accepted them.

## Boundary

This decision amends the §4 realization. It does not accept the repaired workplan, close the Review's blockers, or authorize D4. The repaired workplan requires fresh independent workplan-level Review.
