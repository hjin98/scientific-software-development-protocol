# 7.2.1 brief: find the root issues, then reduce

## 1. Design rule

A change enters 7.2.1 only if it follows from a principle a stronger model or a human would also find correct and useful. A clause that exists only because a weak executor failed in one specific way is rejected. Preference order: delete; fold into a principle; reword in place; add last. **Moving a governed obligation off the entrypoint is not a reduction** (section 3, finding B1): on ordinary routes the entrypoint is the only consumed surface.

## 3. Root issues, as amended by the review

**R1. Inline-checklist framing, and a buried relief.** Four textual causes of narration where nothing is owed (7 of the 10 Q4d hits are a bare "no material findings / findings: none" line; 3 carry an envelope): the section is framed as an end-of-task checklist ("Before you finish, do each that applies", fragment line 13); "findings always" is scoped to delegate answers but reads as a rule for the agent's own report (line 11); "Small, deterministic and local work is included" (line 3) pulls trivial work into scope; the change-only relief sits mid-item 1 (line 14), after the obligations. All four are fixable by **alteration**: hoist the relief into the scope sentence (keeping its delegate exception: answers a delegator asked for and gaps owed for delegates stay owed), drop the checklist frame, scope "findings always" to delegate answers.

**R2. One principle does not derive the whole section.** "Make the status of a scientific result recoverable" explains findings, null envelope, variants, tensions and choices, but not: action ordering before irreversible loss (inquiry before discarding), the resource-budget limit, authority semantics (binding vs origin, "an instruction is not ratification", technical review cannot accept beyond-deliverable items), write-authority and persistence fields, the (a)-(c) blocking rule, or the delegation-request mechanics. These are normative decisions and are named as separate facets, not forced under the principle.

**R3. Not identifiable, not in the core edit.** The closure sentence (line 44) is identical in all arms and the README edit rate is similar in all arms (13 of 17, about 17 of 19, about 12 of 13). 7.2.0 element 4 already says "Product changes need accepted authority" and did not reduce mutations. Rewording line 44 would also break the equality tests between the 7.2.0 entrypoints and 6.6 outside the block. It is dropped from the core edit; if wanted, it is an ablation arm of its own. The silent out-of-scope edits are recorded as a report-completeness symptom (I5b).

**R4. Proportionality is carried by the hoisted relief,** not by "report the change and nothing more": that wording would delete content that is owed (E010 changes a definition and gives an element-6 choices disclosure that is arguably owed).

## 4. Treatments (rev 4)

**T1. Reword in place, on the entrypoint; no relocation.** Shrink by rewording the frozen meanings, not by moving them to the owner. Shape: one scope sentence with the relief hoisted into it; the checklist frame removed; per facet, one principle sentence plus the frozen minimum meaning in compressed wording; the delegate-request questions kept **verbatim** (they exist only on the entrypoint and produce the delegated win); facets named separately where the principle does not derive them (R2). Any meaning dropped from the entrypoint needs a governed label-table amendment (section 6, decision D1). Target: a smaller block, not a principle-only block; compress prose, keep the concrete enumerations that carry the wins.

**T2. Dropped from the core edit** (R3).

**T3. No edit** for I3, I5, I6 (arm-invariant), I7-I10, I11, I12. I1 is waiting on decision D2.

## 5. Design considerations carried into the edit

- Hypothesis to test, not assume: 7.2.0's wins come from enumerated, inline, concrete text ("structured inline blocks work, prose-conditional fails" in the 7.0 to 7.1 lineage; owner reads were not honoured even with stronger wording). A principle-only rewrite may lose them. The stakeholder's directive (principle over patches) and this evidence point in different directions; the compromise in T1 is principle-led framing with enumerations kept where they carry measured wins.
- Size remains soft, losslessness hard (SD-R13).

## 6. Stakeholder decisions needed before implementation

**D1. Reopen the frozen consumed-surface sufficiency?** The consolidated 7.0 workplan froze that agents on ordinary routes read no owner beyond the invoked entrypoint (§8.3, line 633; label table lines 602-627, 21 or more meanings the entrypoint must carry; changes need a governed workplan change). The tests enforce it (`tests/test_protocol_72_scientific_checks.py`, the mapping, no mandatory owner read, entrypoints equal to 6.6 outside the block). Rewording in place does not reopen it. Dropping or relocating any meaning does, and then needs a ratified label-table amendment and updated tests and `frozen-minimum-mapping.json`, each independently reviewed. Recommendation: reword in place only (no reopen) for 7.2.1.

**D2. Doctrine for local choices and run reviews.** The owed-inquiry reader, questions and materiality basis come from 7.0 section 4.3 for run/review work, not from the "local change" extension; "reader/questions and short form (element 6)" is a frozen label meaning. Relaxing the standard to a principle-led reading would turn an all-arms null into an apparent gain. Decide before any probe result, with a rationale independent of pass rates. The frozen rubric stays the primary pre-registered I1 measure; a principle-led reading is secondary and labelled; any re-keyed rubric would be authored by a non-blind analyst and disclosed as such. Recommendation: do not change the doctrine for 7.2.1; measure I1 by component.

**D3. What the end decision is.** With no blind campaign, a 7.2.1 freeze can only be NOT QUALIFIED or stakeholder-ratified on adversarial-review evidence. `PROTOCOL-RELEASE-STATE.yaml` stays untouched until that ratification. Name which outcome is sought, so the stopping rule has something to stop against.

## 7. Implementation and acceptance

- **Who implements:** a fresh context that has **not** seen the 72b evaluator evidence or reports; it receives the principles and the root issues, not candidate sentences, to limit overfitting before the non-blind 72b check. Any later round needs fresh episodes: the dev corpus is spent after this probe.
- **Identity:** a dedicated dev branch and commit; the build requires `\d+\.\d+\.\d+` (`build_skills.py` line 270), so the version is `7.2.1` marked not-frozen, not "7.2.1-dev"; arm extraction needs a committed ref with a matching version; the Orchestrator snapshot profile matches (`generate_protocol_snapshot.py`). Decide the arm name and package digest in the probe plan.
- **Governance (AGENTS.md):** the acceptance workflow applies to any source or dist commit used as an arm; the tests, `frozen-minimum-mapping.json` and any label-table amendment are governed changes with independent review; CHANGELOG, README closeout, Orchestrator snapshot and committed-distribution parity at freeze.
- **Acceptance of the edit:** (1) every frozen meaning of the removed or reworded text maps to the entrypoint, or to a ratified label-table amendment ("entrypoint or owner" is not enough); (2) derivability: no remaining sentence repeats the principle only; (3) size reported against 7.2.0 and 6.6, Q5c static margin holds (`prepare_campaign.py`, 512 B margin, not `measure_blocks.py`); (4) `validate_packages.py` and the tests pass, or their amendments are in the governed set; (5) the reviewer lists any clause that exists to counter one observed failure only.

## 8. Risks

1. Compressing the concrete text may lose the wins (tension, delegated, variants, O1). The probe has an A/A arm to see this above noise.
2. A principle-led hoist may not reduce the narration (7 of 10 hits are a bare "none" line, which the checklist frame invites). Measured.
3. Provider drift, tiny per-class samples and proxy measures limit what the dev probe can show (plan section 3). A null result is a null, not a pass.
4. The root-issue hypotheses came from reading reports and evidence strings of a weak executor and a weak evaluator; the review falsified several. Treat the rest as hypotheses.
