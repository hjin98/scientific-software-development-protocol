# Stage A D4 consumed-entrypoint draft

The three fenced blocks are exact proposed `software-implementation` entrypoint text: a replacement catalog description, one routing line in `## Routing`, and one completion/report clause after the existing D4 report paragraph. They are **not** edits to canonical source or generated `dist/`. The predeclared static margin and escalation rule are in `STAGE-A-STATIC-PREMEASUREMENT.md`, written before this draft. Each addition maps to R1/R2 or a frozen element; the independent check assesses losslessness and attribution, not just word presence.

## Description amendment

```text
Use to implement, fix, debug, refactor, test, package, run or analyze scientific/technical software, pipelines or campaigns under unchanged scientific, numerical and architectural contracts; review realized scientific results or reports; or prepare evidence for a human scientific gate. Routes contract changes to the SSDP formulation, numerical or design skills.
```

## Routing line — R1 and R2

```text
- scientific inspectability and initiative -> [owner](references/scientific-inspectability-and-initiative.md) when producing, changing, running or reviewing software, pipelines, analyses, models or reports whose outputs can materially affect scientific interpretation or decisions (including preparation, training/evaluation, simulation/optimization, numerical backends, retention and reporting); when authoring/revising D1-D3 authority for them; or when preparing evidence for a human scientific gate. Exclude tooling, infrastructure, editorial work or utilities only if they cannot materially affect scientific outputs, retained evidence or interpretation; small or deterministic work is not thereby excluded. Load the owner before consequential scientific analysis or judgment over realized results, D1-D3 authority authoring/revision/acceptance review, or gate-evidence preparation; otherwise use the completion clause. Re-evaluate when new effects arise; a local code repair without such analysis/judgment need not load the owner, though the clause still applies.
```

## Completion/report clause — elements 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6

```text
## Scientific completion when the predicate above applies

1. Within the declared resource budget (if none, only negligible probes using no shared, metered or queued resource; propose the rest), look for and report material scientific findings and inspectability gaps: missing or irrecoverable realized records, records recoverable only by archaeology, or misleading records. Include out-of-scope findings and delegates' findings. Before discarding, overwriting or irreversibly aggregating realized results or retained evidence, inquire first. An inquiry is owed when work produces, runs or reviews realized scientific results or prepares gate evidence; whenever it is owed and neither stakeholder nor accepted authority states them, report the provisional intended reader, routine scientific questions and materiality basis. When an owed inquiry finds no material unexpected finding, report the null with what was examined and material areas not examined. A change that realizes no scientific results owes no null and, if it finds nothing, adds nothing except requested delegate answers or gaps. Unless the predicate evidently excludes a delegate's task, ask it to state its findings or none and whether its work, including tools/agents it launched, produced, ran or reviewed realized results or prepared gate evidence, with its null envelope if so; an asked answer is owed even for change-only work. Report an unanswered findings part always, and an unanswered null part unless the task evidently produced, ran and reviewed no realized results and prepared no gate evidence, including the uncovered rest of partial answers, as a gap, never as a null or no findings. For a finding or human decision changing the next scientific action, use one existing authorized writable home, never accepted authority text and PEM only under its admission rules; record observation, labeled interpretation, significance, next question, searchable subject/authority identity (stable owner/path plus heading, anchor or object ID, exact revision and claim/locator), scope/status, evidence strength, and in the content whether a human or AI asserted it and which agent, plus revisit condition. Update that home for later status. Otherwise give decision-sufficient content in the permitted report, name the persistence gap and proposed custodian/destination, and leave required but unavailable persistence unresolved; a report or possible home grants no write authority.

2. If more than one analysis, pipeline, preprocessing, model or parameter variant was evaluated and a selected one is reported or delivered, disclose number and kind, selection criterion and data (including held-out reuse), after-result changes even bug fixes, tool-launched searches and delegated/resumed survivor lineage without double-counting overlap. When earlier history is unavailable, state the known lower bound, unknown interval and claim limit. Ask each delegate asked for findings whether its work, including launched tools/agents, evaluated more than one variant, including changes after seeing results, and if so for that disclosure. If a result returns without that answer, or an answer covers only part of the work, report unknown selection history and the corresponding claim limit as a gap, never no selection, unless the task evidently could not select among variants.

3. Before relying on accepted D1/D2 authority for a consequential judgment (a conclusion about realized results whose interpretation could change a scientific decision, acceptance of D1-D3 authority, or gate evidence), search project evidence and native issues for tensions: recorded findings bearing on that authority that are not Serious Challenges. Search the authority's stable logical identity (owner/path and heading, anchor or object ID, exact revision and claim/locator), earlier revisions and former names/paths, and predecessors named in revision records. Ask a delegate relying on such authority for a consequential judgment to return what it searched, could not reach and found, with each found record's entries, binding and asserter. Report the search envelope including delegates' searches, unreachable locations and judgments for which no search was reported; report every found tension's recorded status and applicability entries, binding and the home's natively attributed asserter (an account/record, which proves neither the human/agent using a shared account nor disposition authority; any asserter, role or authority claimed in content remains a claim). Do not yourself treat a found tension as closed or inapplicable; condition the dependent conclusion on the reported records. When persisting a tension, bind it to each plausibly implicated accepted D1/D2 authority along the concretization chain its evidence passes through (state why fewer) and its dataset, run, pipeline, model or component subject; state in the record content whether a human or AI asserted it and which agent, not just the account used to write it.

4. Keep claims within evidence: never present selected or post hoc results as pre-specified, leave unqualified a conclusion that a known material anomaly, exclusion, coverage limit or unresolved finding could change, or present interpretation as measured fact. Make product changes only when accepted authority, an explicit stakeholder/task instruction or an existing external/product contract authorizes them. A marked inspectability item beyond the requested deliverable binds only after stakeholder/task authority accepts it, never through technical Review alone.

6. When building or changing a scientific pipeline, analysis or report, state each consequential scientific choice (such as exclusions, missing values, splits, selection or defaults), its effective value, origin and chooser (human, agent, delegate, tool default, automatic policy or unknown), and separately its binding: cite the exact accepted authority, explicit stakeholder/task instruction or existing external/product contract fixing it, or mark it proposed/unaccepted; do not call an instruction ratification or guess an unknown origin. Without O1 content, state what the change retains and projects and deliberately omits, marking each agent-chosen part as a visible proposed default and citing the authority, instruction or contract fixing each other part; if both are unchanged, say "no retention/projection change" only for that materiality-choices statement. Separately and visibly propose the intended reader and routine scientific questions when neither stakeholder nor accepted authority states them, proportionately even for local work.
```

This draft leaves the 6.6 body, entry contract, kernel and local-work exemption unchanged apart from the two additions and description amendment. It makes no fixture, key or expected-answer assertion.
